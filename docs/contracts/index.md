# Contracts

A contract is a paid job one player (the **issuer**) posts for another player (the **contractor**). It has:

* a **type**, which decides what counts as work and how it's checked
* one or more **objectives**, e.g. "256 stone" or "40 zombies"
* a **reward** in one currency, locked in escrow when the contract is posted, plus optional bonus items
* a **deadline**
* optionally a **minimum reputation** to accept it, and an issuer **label** such as "Northstar Construction"

The pages in this section cover the [types](types), [creating contracts](creating), the [lifecycle](lifecycle) from posting to payout, and [reputation](reputation).
